APPARATIONS IN OUTER SPACE

1. Question Mark

The question mark in space is a real cosmic alignment captured by NASA's James Webb Space Telescope (JWST). It is an optical illusion caused by the alignment and gravitational interactions of incredibly distant galaxies billions of light-years away.Astronomers have actually identified two different major instances of a "question mark" structure in recent deep-space images.

2. Black Hole

The closest known black hole to Earth is Gaia BH1, located approximately 1,560 light-years away in the constellation Ophiuchus. It is a dormant stellar-mass black hole roughly 10 times the mass of the Sun, and it poses no threat to our planet.

3. Andromeda Galaxy

The Andromeda Galaxy (Messier 31 or M31) is a massive, barred spiral galaxy located approximately 2.5 million light-years from Earth, making it the nearest major galactic neighbor to our own Milky Way. It is the most distant celestial object visible from Earth with the naked eye under dark, moonless skies.
